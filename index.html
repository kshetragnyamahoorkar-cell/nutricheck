🧠 Loomis Method Drawing Assistant

Steps

1. Draw the Ball (Cranium)

Draw a circle or ball for the skull.

2. Add the Jaw Line

Draw the lower face from the bottom of the ball.

3. Center Line + Horizon

Draw vertical center line and horizontal eye line.

4. Divide the Face

Eye line is at ½ of the head.
Nose at ½ between eye line and chin.
Mouth at ⅓ between nose and chin.
